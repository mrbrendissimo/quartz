## The Grand Dame

- **Games**
	- (1) golden goose = 10gp. Divide among group. 
	- Cash in at the end. Player with most GG gets a role on magic table
		- Everyone 100gg ea.
 
- Gyp 
	- Min 2gg buy-in
		- 2d6
		- 7 or 12 win, anything else = lose
		- Double bet to add 1d6
		- Payout 3:2

- **Blackdice**
	- Buy-in (min 1), but equal for all players 
	- Ea. player has 8d6
	- Lowest score eliminated
	- Die roll of 6 is included in score, and then removed for the game

- **Orcish Auction**
	- Each player buys in to the game with 3gg. Five players to a table.
	- At the start of each round, you must each choose a number between 1 and 5. 
	- At the end of the round, you each reveal your chosen number. 
	- The player with the lowest number not chosen by any other player wins 1gg  multiplied by the number they chose. 
	- The game continues for three rounds.

- **Crackshot**
	- Each player buys into the game with 5gg.
	- You begin with 16 health.
	- At the start of each round, the house rolls 2d10, to set the house score. 
	- Each player then rolls their choice of 2d6, 3d6, or 4d6, the total is their score. 
	- The difference between your score and the house score is how many points of health you lose. 
	- If you lose all health, you are eliminated. 
	- The last remaining player wins 4gg multiplied by the number of players at the start. 
	- If you score exactly the house score on any round, you win 2gg instantly.

- **Roulette**
	- Odd or Even (2:1)
	- Corner (5:1)
	- Split (10:1)
	- Straight (20:1)

- **Skeleton Slots** 
	- 1gg to play 
	- Roll one die at a time starting with d4
	- 4 or more lose. 3 or less collecting winnings or continue
	- d4 = break even
	- d6 = 1gg
	- d8 = 2gg
	- d10 = 5gg
	- d12 = 10gg
	-  d20 = 20gg


## The Sewers
- General Statistics  
	- ***Floors.***
		- All vertical surfaces are grimy and slippery. DC 10 Dexterity saving throw, or fall prone at end of movement
		- Can bypass by moving half their movement speed
	- ***Illumination.***
		- Pitch black in the sewers
	- ***Sewer Water.***
		- 5ft deep unless otherwise noted.
		- Visibility reduced to 10ft 
		- First time entering water, DC 11 Constitution, or contract [[diseases#Sewer Plague|Sewer Plague]] 
	- Wall and Ceilings
		- Tunnels and rooms in the sewer are made of old stonework. Ceilings are 8ft above the gangways.

## S1. Control Office
- **First Glance:** 
	- Room with handwheels and levers
	- Desk and two chairs
	- Half dissolved skeleton on the ground

- **Details:** 
	- ***Control Mechanisms.*** Used for opening and closing the waterpipes flow. Worn labels are not legible. Levers are lightly color coded yellow and orange. Four handwheels are rusted, but appear usable. 
	- DC 13 Investigation check to figure out how they work. Failure, they think they know how they work, but get tcontrols reversed.

- **What's Hidden**
	-  Handwheels let more water in (1ft per minute)
	- Levers drain the water (1ft per minute)
	- Emergency drains in **S4** prevent the sewer from overflowing
	- ***Treasure:*** Set of [[masons-tools-xphb|Mason's Tools]] and 5 gp

- **Creatures:** 
	- If a character uses an action to turn handwheel or use levers, 
		- (4) [[psychic-gray-ooze-xmm|Psychic Gray Ooze]] 
		- (3) [[kuo-toa-xmm|Kuo-toa]] and (1) [[kuo-toa-whip-xmm|Kuo-toa Whip]] swim over to investigate three rounds later.

## Aboleth's Lair

- **First Glance:** 
	- Walkways are 15ft above the floor, and 15ft below the ceiling
	- The water just barely reaches the edge of the walkways.
	- The waterpipes (5e) on each side of the chamber are closed, as are the drainages (3c) underneath the water. 
		- Used to transport the nobles abducted
	- By both of the handwheels on either side of the chamber (4d) stand one merrow mindslave- A stone building (6f) that rises up to the ceiling.  Its steel door is closed, but unlocked. Treasure is inside here

- **Details:** 
	- The aboleth wants the party as far away from the exit as possible, before it orders its mindslaves to flood the chamber with sewer water. 

- **What's Hidden**
	-  When the party arrive at the aboleth’s lair, it remains hidden deep in the murky water.

- **Creatures:** 
	- On [[merrow-xmm|Merrows]] first action begin flooding chamber. Then ready a grapple.
		- Free the slaves. Each time a merrow takes damage, DC 14 Wisdom saving throw against the aboleth’s enslavement.
	- If the fight is souring for it, Oosith commands any remainingg mindslaves to open one of the drainages, and swims out

- **Flooding the Chamber**
	- ***Water Level***
		- Adjust level at top of round
		- 30ft = room completely filled
		- 15ft = aboleth underwater
		- 7ft = exposed, but aura
		- 1ft = exposed, no aura
	- Control
		- (4d) ea. (+)5ft water/round
		- (2b) ea. (-)5ft water/round

- **Underwater Combat** 
	- ***Movement***
		- While you’re swimming, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot
	- ***Impeded Weapons***
		- When making a melee attack roll with a weapon underwater, a creature that lacks a [[swim-speed-xphb|Swim Speed]] has [[disadvantage-xphb|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll unless the weapon deals Piercing damage.
		- A ranged attack roll with a weapon underwater automatically misses a target beyond the weapon’s normal range, and the attack roll has Disadvantage against a target within normal range.
		- Anything underwater has [[3-Mechanics/CLI/variant-rules/resistance-xphb|Resistance]] to Fire damage
	- ***Suffocation***
		- A creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 1 plus its Constitution modifier (minimum of 30 seconds) before suffocation begins. When a creature runs out of breath or is choking, it gains 1 Exhaustion level at the end of each of its turns. When a creature can breathe again, it removes all levels of Exhaustion it gained from suffocating.
