# Part 1 - Windy Valley

- Windy Valley is located South of Bryn Shander
- Windy Valley was an abandoned town taken over by people mind controlled by brain-slugs.
- The town was rebuilt overnight, and residents kidnap travelers to add to their collective
- All villagers where hats to conceal the slugs. They are unnervingly nice.
- Salt is outlawed within the town

>[! check]+ Town Clues 
> - If anyone that loses a hat, reveals the slug.
> - If slug is removed, liberated person tells PCs everything and aids them 
> - Village will eventually surround players and force a hat on them
> - Slugs are in endless supply via the well
> - The old man, Bash, is the only non-possessed citizen. Hordes salt in the windmill 

## Entrance
- When entering the town, PCs are stopped by the Constable Reggie, and his checkpoint
- Reggie is wearing a bobby with a gold star.
- Behind Reggie is large metal cage with a metal table in the middle
- He checks the PCs items, pulls out any rations, burns them, and hands the party back their stuff
- Tells PCs to head to the tavern, the mad hatter, for a free drink. And not to forget to get a hat at the Dashing Haberdashery 

## Tavern
- Outside, the tavern has a sign "The Flying Bat", but the bottom/top of "B" have been painted over
- Everyone inside is wearing a hat. There is a synchronized dance happening with all patrons
- First drink is free, and PCs make up price after that
- Bartender is Avery. Avery was a courier, until recently, but was charmed by the hats and stayed.

## Haberdashery/Factory
- Small store front. Large factory in back
- Factory is full of villagers robotically working on hats and filling them with slugs
- Villagers cycle through with buckets from the well

## Any Location
- A man runs past PCs. He has wild hat hair and no hat 
	- He is being chased by three villagers, one carrying a top hat, saying "Sir, don't forget your hat! "
	- PCs later see the man wearing the hat and saying, "Thank goodness I nearly lost it!"
- Barrels are filled with ectoplasm to feed slugs. Villagers dunk their heads
- At nightfall, torch-wielding mob of villagers form search party to find PCs.
