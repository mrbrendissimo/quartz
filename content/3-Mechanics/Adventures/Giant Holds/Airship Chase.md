
### Skill Challenge
 - Contest rolls - airship vs. dragon
 - 3 Successes Win  or 3 failures
 - At most 5 rounds
 - Deadly, and one-shots

### Rounds 
 - Where: players describe where they are (can move between rounds)
 - Hazard: DM describes hazard or obstacle in the way 
 - Skill: Players pick skill to overcome that challenge 
 - Dragon and airship roll that skill - either success or failure
 - Dragon gets legendary action

### Iymrith Actions 

- Frightful Presence 
	- Any NPC in the targeted station is unable to participate next round
	- Players in the location make DC23 Wisdom save, failure cannot move to a new station next round
- Wing Attack 
	- Any NPC in the targeted station is knocked unconscious
	- Players in the location make a DC23 Dexterity save, failure 16 dmg and cannot move next round
		- Players can use 3/4 cover rule below deck
- Gargoyle Attack 
	- A gargoyle attacks the targeted station with the goal of killing everyone there 
	- If players *are not* there , any NPC in station is killed, and gargoyle leaves
	- If players *are* there, simple theater of the mind combat
- Breath Weapon
	- The targeted station is destroyed and becomes inoperable
	- Any NPC in the targeted station is killed.
	- Players in the station make a DC23 Dex saving throw, taking 88 lightning damage on fail, or half as much damage on success
