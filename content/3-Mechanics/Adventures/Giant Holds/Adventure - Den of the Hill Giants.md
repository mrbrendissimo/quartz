## Grudd Haug Features

> [!note] Grudd Haug: General Features
> 
> **Ceilings.** 30-foot-high ceilings
> 
> **Climbing.** Walls of piled timber have many handholds and footholds and can be climbed with a successful DC 10 Strength ([Athletics](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Athletics)) check. Other sloped and vertical surfaces in Grudd Haug, including mud walls and log roofs, require a successful DC 15 Strength ([Athletics](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Athletics)) check to climb.
> 
> **Curtains.** The curtains are thick enough to intercept arrows. A curtain can be pulled down with a successful DC 13 Strength ([Athletics](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Athletics)) check.
> 
> **Illumination.** The gong tower (area 8) and the stockyard (area 9) get plenty of natural light. Oil lamps dimly illuminate rooms and caves that have no windows.
> 
> **Oversized Furnishings and Objects.** Most of the furnishings and other items in Grudd Haug are sized for hill giants.  Giant-sized furniture, difficult terrain.

## 1. Main Entrance

- **First Glance:** 
	- Muddy trail ends doorway blocked by a boulder
	- North of entrance is a 60ft tall outcropping of rock with flat, lopped-off peak

- **Details:** 
	- Outcropping climbed  with a successful DC 15 Strength ([Athletics](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Athletics)) check

- **What's Hidden**
	-  The boulder is smaller than the doorway. 
		- Small and Medium creatures can move around it unimpeded
		- A character can push the boulder 10 feet on flat ground by using an action and succeeding on a DC 22 Strength ([Athletics](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Athletics)) check.

- **Creatures:** 
	- [[hill-giant-xmm|Hill Giant]] 
	- Area 4 [[orc-xphb|Orcs]] may investigate area

## 2. Feasting Hall 

- **First Glance:** 
	-  Oil lamps in iron sconces are mounted to thick wooden pillars that support rafters 30 feet from the floor and the roof, which peaks 65 feet overhead
	- 20-foot-high wooden ledge that the goblins use to get around the room.
	- A wooden ladder climbs up to the ledge, and windows a couple of feet above the ledge let shafts of natural light into the hall.
	- Eight sturdy but battered wooden tables covered with globs of grease, gnawed bones, empty casks, and scraps of food stand

- **Details:** 
	- Refuse covers the dried mud floor, in the middle of which are two 20-foot-diameter circular holes topped with crisscrossing wooden beams held in place by mud cement. 
	- (2) 20-foot-diameter circular holes topped with crisscrossing wooden beams held in place by mud cement. The beams are far enough apart that a Medium or smaller creature can slip between them without having to squeeze. A horrible stench rises from the holes, accompanied by the squealing of pigs (see area 12).
	- [Chief Guh](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/chief-guh-skt.md) lies at the west end of the hall, slumped atop a four-wheeled, flatbed wagon that bends and creaks under her great bulk.
	- Piled around the wagon are bones and other refuse from Guh's recent meals
	
- **What's Hidden**
	- The wagon's axles are cracked and bowed, its wheels canted inward. 
	- Any character who studies Guh's "throne" can, with a successful DC 10 Intelligence check, figure out how to sever a wagon axle with a single hit, causing the wagon bed to fall and spilling Guh onto the floor. 
	- Hitting the axle just right requires a successful melee or ranged attack against AC 15 that deals 7 or more damage. Sends Guh tumbling into the nearest pit (3d6 fall damage)
	- Once Guh is toppled from her "throne," characters can spot the valuables hidden therein (see "Treasure").

- **Creatures:** 
	- [Chief Guh](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/chief-guh-skt.md)

## 8. Gong Tower

- **First Glance:** 
	- Central to the den's defense is this 60-foot-tall log tower perched atop an 80-foot-high bluff overlooking the river. 
	- A wooden ladder sized for Medium humanoids is lashed to the south side of the tower.

- **Details:** 
	- Hanging from the tower's rafters is a brass gong with a wooden mallet.

- **What's Hidden**
	-  They have a fairly unobstructed view of the countryside out to a range of 1 mile, though there are hillocks and trees behind which creatures can hide. 
	- If the guards spot any unusual creatures approaching the den, or if they come under attack, one of the hobgoblins strikes the gong.
	- The ringing of the gong is loud enough to put the entire den on alert (see the Grudd Haug Roster table for notes on how the den inhabitants react). 
	- The ringing of the gong also causes [Chief Guh](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/chief-guh-skt.md) to bellow, "Lunch time! Me want food!"

- **Creatures:** 
	- (4) [hobgoblins](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/hobgoblin-warrior-xmm.md) stand guard in the tower,

- **Treasure:** 
	- Each hobgoblin carries `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`d10`) gp in a pouch.

## 9. Stockyard

- **First Glance:** 
	- Five crude tents stand in the middle of this muddy yard, surrounding a campfire.

- **Details:** 
	- watch over the one hundred [sheep](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/beast/sheep-skt.md) that are penned in the yard, and they remain here when an alarm sounds. 
	- North of the stockyard, a mud trail climbs a ramp and curves back toward area 1. At the bottom of the ramp, at the base of a 60-foot-tall stone bluff, is the entrance of a tunnel that leads north to areas 10 and 11. To the west, a wide opening in the log dam leads to area 12. 

- **What's Hidden**
	-  A palisade of 20-foot-tall sharpened logs encloses the stockyard, except for an opening to the southeast with a giant boulder outside. 
	- The boulder, if rolled into place, is just big enough to fill the opening.
	- A character can push the boulder 10 feet across flat ground by using an action and succeeding on a DC 22 Strength ([Athletics](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Athletics)) check. A hill giant can do it without needing to make the check.

- **Creatures:** 
	- [orcs](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/tough-xmm.md) of the Iceshield tribe are gathered here

## 10. Giants' Caves

	- They also awaken if attacked. They don't wake up to the sound of other creatures talking in the room or moving about. 

- **Creatures:**
