# Griffon Egg


- **Weight**: 200.0 lbs.

This 3-foot-diameter, 4-foot-high egg weighs 200 pounds.

After `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`d6`) weeks, a Medium baby griffon noncombatant hatches from the egg. After 1 year, the creature becomes a [griffon companion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/beast/griffon-companion-whereevillives.md). The GM can advance or slow this timeline to suit the campaign's pacing.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 99*
