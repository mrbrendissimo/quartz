# Battle Master's Compendium



Written by [Bloodlord Varrox](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/bloodlord-varrox-whereevillives.md), Battle Master's Compendium is a military treatise. Each of its thirteen chapters covers a different skill related to military strategy and tactics. The book's detailed analysis covers military units, weapons, strategy, rank, discipline, and much more.

When you study a chapter in the book for 1 hour during a short or long rest, you learn interesting military strategies. The first time you make an Intelligence or Charisma check to influence a general, monarch, or other military leader within the next 48 hours, you have advantage on the check.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 73*
