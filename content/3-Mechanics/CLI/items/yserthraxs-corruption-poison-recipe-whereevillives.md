# Yserthrax's Corruption Poison Recipe
*Adventuring gear, poison (injury)*  


- **Properties**: [Poison](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Poison)

This one-page recipe is written in Undercommon on a piece of bloodstained vellum. If you are proficient with [alchemist's supplies](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/alchemists-supplies-xphb.md) or a [poisoner's kit](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/poisoners-kit-xphb.md), you can make a dose of this poison with raw materials worth 750 gp and 1 hour of uninterrupted work.

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 1 minute. They can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns to end the [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition. While [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) in this way, the creature takes `dice:3d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(10)` (`3d6`) force damage at the start of each of their turns.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 256*
