# Treasure Rat
*Adventuring gear*  



When you open the door to this white-furred, red-eyed rat's cage, they point their nose in the direction of the nearest magic item, if any, within 30 feet of them that isn't being worn or carried by another creature. They continue pointing their nose at that item for 1 minute or until you shut the cage door. After pointing at a magic item, the rat refuses to do so again until they consume a piece of cheese worth at least 10 gp.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 51*
