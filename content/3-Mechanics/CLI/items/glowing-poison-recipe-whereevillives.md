# Glowing Poison Recipe
*Adventuring gear*  



This one-page recipe is written in fine calligraphy on a sheet of clean white parchment. If you are proficient with [alchemist's supplies](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/alchemists-supplies-xphb.md) or a [poisoner's kit](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/poisoners-kit-xphb.md), you can make a dose of this poison with raw materials worth 250 gp and 1 hour of uninterrupted work.

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 8 hours. The DC for this saving throw equals 8 + your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus. While [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) in this way, a creature sheds bright green light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. Any attack roll against the [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) creature has advantage if the attacker can see them, and the affected creature can't benefit from being [invisible](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Invisible) or hide from creatures who can see.

Additionally, the creature leaves a glowing green trail whenever they move. The trail lasts until the creature is no longer [poisoned](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned).

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 21*
