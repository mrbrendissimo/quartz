# Sun Eater Staff
*Staff, weapon, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*  
![](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/sun-eater-staff.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d6 bludgeoning
  - Two-handed: 1d8 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Versatile](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

This lengthy staff of black wood has veins of black opal and obsidian running through it, and it feels cool to the touch.

This staff can be wielded as a magic quarterstaff that grants a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.

The staff has 10 charges and regains `dice:1d6+4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6 + 4`) charges daily at midnight.

## Spells

While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC: [acid arrow](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/melfs-acid-arrow-xphb.md) (2 charges), [blight](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/blight-xphb.md) (4 charges), [darkness](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/darkness-xphb.md) (1 charge), [fog cloud](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/fog-cloud-xphb.md) (1 charge), or [wall of force](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/wall-of-force-xphb.md) (5 charges).

## Consume Light

You can expend 1 charge as an action to extinguish any light sources of your choice that you can see within 120 feet of you.

## Fire Immunity

When you take fire damage while holding the staff, you can expend 1 charge as a reaction.

You gain immunity to fire damage until the start of your next turn, including against the triggering effect.
