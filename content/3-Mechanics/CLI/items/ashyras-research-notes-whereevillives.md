# Ashyra's Research Notes
*Adventuring gear*  



Ashyra's research notes about magic and necromancy are organized and indexed. When you fail an Intelligence ([Arcana](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Arcana)) check to recall lore, you can spend 1 hour during a short or long rest reviewing the notes and repeat the check. If the check is related to necromancy magic, you have advantage when you reroll it.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 171*
