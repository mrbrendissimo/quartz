# Heavenkiller Chronicles
*Adventuring gear*  



Spicule's vast tome contains secrets concerning celestials and how to use them to fuel dark magic. You must spend 50 hours studying this tome to gain the following benefits:

- You have advantage on Intelligence checks to determine the vulnerabilities and weaknesses of Celestials.  
- You deal an extra `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg|text(9)` (`2d8`) damage to Celestials when you hit them with an attack.  
- You know how to harvest parts of Celestials and use them to fuel dark magic. You can harvest a heart, eye, or other useful organ from a Celestial's corpse with a successful DC 16 Wisdom ([Survival](/3-Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Survival)) check. You can use a harvested Celestial organ in place of one material component that would normally be consumed by the spell, up to a cost of 2,500 gp.  

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 241*
