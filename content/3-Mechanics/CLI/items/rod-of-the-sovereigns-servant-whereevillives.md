# Rod of the Sovereigns' Servant
*Rod, very rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*  
![](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/rod-of-the-sovereigns-servant.webp#right)


While holding this rod of blackened bone, you can cast the [speak with dead](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/speak-with-dead-xphb.md) spell without expending a spell slot.

Additionally, while you hold the rod, you can cast the following spells using your spell save DC: [animate dead](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/animate-dead-xphb.md) as a 5th-level spell, [bestow curse](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/bestow-curse-xphb.md), [geas](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/geas-xphb.md), and [raise dead](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/raise-dead-xphb.md). Once the rod has been used to cast one of these four spells, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dusk.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 171*
