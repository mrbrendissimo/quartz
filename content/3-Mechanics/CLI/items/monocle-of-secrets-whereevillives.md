# Monocle of Secrets
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/monocle-of-secrets.webp#right)


While you wear this gold-rimmed monocle, you have advantage on ability checks made to find secret doors and traps.

Additionally, you can use an action while holding a mundane object to learn that object's exact value, the materials used to create it, and whether it is genuine or counterfeit.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 21*
