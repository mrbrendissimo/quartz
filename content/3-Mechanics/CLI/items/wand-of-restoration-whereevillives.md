# Wand of Restoration
*Wand, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/wand-of-restoration.webp#right)


This wand has 6 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 2 of its charges to cast the [lesser restoration](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/lesser-restoration-xphb.md) spell from it, or 4 charges to cast the [greater restoration](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/greater-restoration-xphb.md) spell from it.

The wand regains `dice:1d4+2|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4 + 2`) expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`). On a roll of 1, the wand crumbles into dust and is destroyed.

*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles p. 99*
