# Powerful Impact
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 38*  

**Prerequisite**: Falcon

If the bird descends at least 20 feet on their turn immediately before hitting with a Rending Talons attack or Talons attack, the target takes an extra `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(7)` (`2d6`) bludgeoning damage. (+1 CR)
