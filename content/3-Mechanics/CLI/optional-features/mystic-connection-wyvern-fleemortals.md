# Mystic Connection: Wyvern
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 290*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Beastheart, [Wyvern Companion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/dragon/wyvern-companion-fleemortals.md)

## Acid-Enhanced

As a bonus action, you cause your hands to exude acid for 1 minute. While exuding acid, your melee weapon attacks and unarmed strikes deal an extra `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`d8`) acid damage. Once you use this bonus action, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
