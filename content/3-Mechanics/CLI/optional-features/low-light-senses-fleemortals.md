# Low-Light Senses
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 326*  

**Prerequisite**: Sewer-Dwelling Creature

The sewer creature gains [blindsight](/3-Mechanics/CLI/senses.md#Blindsight) or [darkvision](/3-Mechanics/CLI/senses.md#Darkvision) out to a range of 60 feet.
