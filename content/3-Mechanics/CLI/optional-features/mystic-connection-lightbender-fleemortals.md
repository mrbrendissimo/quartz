# Mystic Connection: Lightbender
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 181*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Beastheart, [Lightbender Companion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/lightbender-companion-fleemortals.md)

## Unleash Sunlight

As a bonus action, you cause your skin to flare with brilliant light. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you who can see you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your exploit save DC or be [blinded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Blinded) for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn). Once you use this bonus action, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
