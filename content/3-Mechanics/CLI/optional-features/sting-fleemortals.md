# Sting
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 42*  

**Prerequisite**: Fire ants, bullet ants, or wasps

*Melee Weapon Attack:* `dice:1d20+4|noform|noparens|text(+4)` to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(2)` (`d4`) piercing damage plus `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(7)` (`2d6`) poison damage, or 1 piercing damage plus `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(3)` (`d6`) poison damage if the swarm has half their hit points or fewer.
