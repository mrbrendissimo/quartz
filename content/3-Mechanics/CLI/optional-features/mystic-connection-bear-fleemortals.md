# Mystic Connection: Bear
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 44*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Beastheart, [Bear Companion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/beast/bear-companion-fleemortals.md)

## Ursine Might

As a bonus action, you empower yourself with the strength of a great bear. For the next minute, your Strength-based attacks deal an extra `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`d10`) damage and you have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws. Once you use this bonus action, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
