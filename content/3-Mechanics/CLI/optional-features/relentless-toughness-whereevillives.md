# Relentless Toughness
*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles*  

The creature is infused with troll essence. If they are reduced to 0 hit points by damage other than acid or fire damage, they are reduced to 1 hit point instead. Once this effect is triggered, the creature can't benefit from this modification again until they finish a long rest.
