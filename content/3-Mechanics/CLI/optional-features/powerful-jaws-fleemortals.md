# Powerful Jaws
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 36*  

**Prerequisite**: Giant panda or sun bear

The bear's Bite attack deals an extra `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg|text(4)` (`d8`) damage, and it deals maximum damage to Plants. (A bear with this feature can't take the Multiattack action.)
