# Mystic Connection: Drangolin
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 179*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Beastheart, [Drangolin Companion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/dragon/drangolin-companion-fleemortals.md)

## Ear to the Ground

As a bonus action, you gain [tremorsense](/3-Mechanics/CLI/senses.md#Tremorsense) out to a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. Once you use this bonus action, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
