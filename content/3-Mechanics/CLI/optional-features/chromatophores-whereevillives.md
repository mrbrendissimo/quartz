# Chromatophores
*Source: Where Evil Lives: The MCDM Book of Boss Battles*  

The creature's skin gains the camouflaging qualities of a squid. As an action, the creature can make themself [invisible](/3-Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Invisible) for 10 minutes or until they attack, cast a spell, or manifest a power. Once they become invisible in this way, they can't do so again until they finish a long rest.
