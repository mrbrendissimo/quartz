	- With Felgolos help, you raid dragon hordes in Ascore
	- Give Felgolos his [Yondalla's Delight](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/1570423-yondallas-delight).

- **The Conical Magical, LLC**_
    - Gundar is under water with the business
    - Gundar needs money to start a distribution company, and cut out Triboar (Zhentarim aka Black Network) from current distribution
    
- _**Let's Buy a Nightstone**_
    - Talurth Nandar tasked you to scope out Nightstone, with an eye towards him outright owning the town
    - Elf Lord wants to claim the wrecked city for himself, and create a cartel to rival the Zhentarim (Black Network)
    - ~~Will need residents. Town is vacant.~~ 
	    - Town is occupied by the Ardeep Elves
    - Need to distract Talurth Nandar
    - Need 50,000 gp in fees, bribes and expenses
    - Need various documents claiming ownership. Forged or otherwise (Triboar is good place to start).
    - Will need a Noble or someone of renown to sponsor you

- _**Mimic Taming**_
    - A captured mimic from the Hunting Lodge in Ardeep Forest shows signs of being domestication. Perhaps it can be tamed.
    - Find someone that knows about mimics to begin taming (Lots of Druids in Goldenfields).
    - Tamed things can be a pet, a familiar or a druid's companion
-  _**Return to Vault of the Devour**_ 
    - A vault door remains closed in the dungeon.
    - Dungeon is located in the remote North in a deserted town called Windy Valley (South of Bryn Shander)
- _**Dank the Bugbear**_
    - One year ago, a circus caravan passed through Phandalin. Without a word, Dank threw off his armor and cast aside his weapons and joined the caravan
    - Dank's act has made an impression on folks that have seen it. Where could he be now?
	    - Felgolos saw Dank's show. Said the circus mysteriously disappeared.
	    - Has anyone heard of a mysterious circus?
