Northshield House is a fine local inn — stately, clean, and quiet. The proprietor, [[Urgala Meltimer]], is a retired adventurer who bought the inn from the Phorndyl family a little over ten years ago, after her wife (a wizard) vanished in the Underdark on an expedition. She much prefers retirement and warns armed guests to keep their blades sheathed during their stay. Urgala has three [[mastiff-xmm|Mastiffs]] that sleep in the ground floor common room, and she employs a staff of six loyal, well-paid commoners. Guests sleep in private rooms on the upper floor.
