>[!Note]+ Read Aloud
>You see an enormous tower floating on a billowy cloud a thousand feet overhead. The tower must be hundreds of feet tall, and its spire looks strangely like a pointy wizard's hat. As the tower drifts closer, stairs made of clouds begin to form underneath it and descend toward you.

- After climbing stairs (human size), reaching tower landing - Zephyros descends from 2nd level with levitate to greet party.
- [[Zephyros]] is an eccentric cloud giant with windswept white hair, a wispy white beard, and a billowy purple robe adorned with gold stars. He poses no threat.
- Greet party and ask if it is okay to depart, to escape dragons

## Zephros Meet and Greet
- He like small folk
- Awhile back, he tried creating a spell and it went wrong. He is now incredibly indecisive
- He casts [[contact-other-plane-xphb|Contact Other Plane]] spell to help him make decisions. 
- If spell fails, it causes insanity. He plucks the insanity out of his brain and puts in his brain jar 
- The ordning has shattered - he is looking for a way to pacify giant kin from destroying the sword coast 

## Ordning 
- Giant hiearchy/caste system.
- Storm Giants are rulers. They live in fortresses under the sea and in the sky
- Cloud giants take orders from Storm giants. Build floating castles on clouds
- Frost Giants, Fire Giants, and Stone Giants take orders from Cloud Giants.
    - Frost > Knightly orders
    - Fire > Armory
    - Stone > Infrastructure
- Hill Giants are dullards and bottom of the food chain. They are bullies and vanguard.

## Ordning Shattered
- Zephyros can feel the ordning has been shattered. All giants have the same feeling.
- It happened several months ago
- This is why there is all the giant chaos and attacks.
- All giants have a gut feeling of lack of purpose. Giants of all types are looking for ways to increase their standing, or keep their standing

## King Hekaton
- Storm Giant [[king-hekaton-skt|King Hekaton]], ruler of giants, lost control of his court
- He was shaken of Storm Giants losing their apex position. Became strong handed with court
- Then Queen Neri, who often visited small folk on remote islands, was found dead. Signs show at the hands of small folk
- King Hekaton started searching for answers, and disappeared

## Contact Other Planes 
- With no ruler and no ordning, Demigods described the Heroes of Phandalin
- Asked Zephyros to find and bring  to [[Bryn Shander]]
- Did not explain for what purpose, but only to be careful to not interfere 
- He has been chasing PCs since Waterdeep, but slow going as he had to be careful around cities
- He asks the players to remain on the first floor and stay away from the 2nd and 3rd floor.

### First Floor

- Dangling from the ceiling by iron chains are six crystal spheres with [continual flame](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/continual-flame-xphb.md) spells cast on them. 
- The illuminated spheres keep the room brightly lit.
- The tower's second floor can be seen through a 20-foot-wide hole in the soaring ceiling.

### Second Floor

- 80-foot-high ceiling and tall, slender windows set with panes of stained glass. 
- Furnishings include a giant-sized bed and an enormous wooden chest 
	- sealed by an [arcane lock](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/arcane-lock-xphb.md) spell. 
- Stone shelves protrude from the walls at heights of 20, 40, and 60 feet, and these shelves bear the weight of [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md)'s vast collection of journals (see "Treasure").
- Floating 10 feet above the floor is a [navigation orb](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/navigation-orb-skt.md) (see appendix B) that [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) uses to control the movement of the tower.
- [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) uses magic to move between levels. 
- A 20-foot-diameter hole in the floor allows access to the first level, the floor of which is 100 feet below. A similarly sized hole in the ceiling leads to the aerie.

#### Treasure

- The wooden chest is 9 feet long, 5 feet wide, and 5 feet tall. 
- It contains an assortment of giant-sized robes, a giant pair of sandals, a large electrum comb encrusted with moonstones (worth 750 gp and weighing 25 pounds)
- [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md)'s spellbook. 
	- The spellbook weighs 250 pounds has gilded silver covers. It contains all the spells that [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) has prepared plus the following additional spells: [arcane eye](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/arcane-eye-xphb.md), [continual flame](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/continual-flame-xphb.md), [fire shield](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/fire-shield-xphb.md), [hypnotic pattern](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/hypnotic-pattern-xphb.md), [mage armor](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/mage-armor-xphb.md), [mirror image](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/mirror-image-xphb.md), [misty step](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/misty-step-xphb.md), [modify memory](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/modify-memory-xphb.md), [shatter](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/shatter-xphb.md), and [slow](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/slow-xphb.md).
	- [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md)'s library contains more than five hundred research journals that he wrote himself. For the past fifty years, [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) has been drifting around the Moonshae Isles and cataloging its many wonders, both magical and mundane. 
		- Each book weighs 100 pounds, consolidates a month's worth of research, and is worth as much as 250 gp.

N'von carries a pouch containing ten pinches of [faerie dust](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/faerie-dust-skt.md). He offers the pouch to [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) as a gift (see "Development").

#### Development

- Battle
	- If a battle erupts between the characters and the cultists, [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) watches the chaos unfold from a safe distance.
	- Only if the characters are near defeat does the cloud giant cast a [mass suggestion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/mass-suggestion-xphb.md) spell, urging them to leave 

- Peaceful 
	- Kraken Emissaries offer Zeph the pouch of faerie dust as a gift and urge him to meet with their cell leader
	- Zeph accepts the gift and allows them to remain in his tower while he considers their offer. He then retreats to the second floor and casts [contact other plane](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/contact-other-plane-xphb.md) to determine the best course of action.  
	- Zeph determines with the aid of the spell that the cult's interests are opposed to his own, and politely asks the [cultists](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/cultist-xmm.md) to leave. 
	- The cultists grudgingly leave, but not before Amarath chastises the cloud giant wizard for his "foolish and shortsighted decision."
	-  [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) feels bad about keeping their gift and gives the pouch of faerie dust to the PCs

## Orb Strike 

>[!Note]+ Read Aloud
> On the tenth day, A huge silver dragon glides through the clouds, approaching quickly. As it flies closer, you see a number of large stone marbles clutched in its talons. The marbles are branded with [[Lords' Alliance|The Lords' Alliance]] emblem.

- PCs have one minute to prepare for arrival
- [[adult-silver-dragon-xmm|Adult Silver Dragon]] throws the drop pods at the castle and roost on top of the castle
- Dwarves emerge from drop pods and drink their [potions of gaseous form](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/potion-of-gaseous-form-xdmg.md)
- [Zephyros](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/zephyros-skt.md) does his best to protect the orb without harming any of the dwarves
	- [charm person](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/charm-person-xphb.md), [Otiluke's resilient sphere](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/otilukes-resilient-sphere-xphb.md), and [mass suggestion](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/mass-suggestion-xphb.md) to trap or divert them. 
	- Meanwhile, the dragon watches the battle from the aerie and tries to neutralize the cloud giant wizard with its paralyzing breath. 
		- ***Paralyzing Breath.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 20. 1st Failure: Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn. 2nd Failure: The target has the Paralyzed condition, and it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.
