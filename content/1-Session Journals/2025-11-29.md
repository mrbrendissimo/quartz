## [[Waterdeep]]

>[!Note]+ Read Aloud
>You find yourself at a familiar villa, House Nandar’s villa in the North Ward. Lord of the house is [Talurth Nandar](app://obsidian.md/Talurth%20Nandar). 
>
>The front of the Nandar villa is an unassuming stone façade set with a pair of dark wooden doors. Embedded on the doors are iron plates embossed with a stone bridge and a flying flag. After a knock and an inspection of you from beyond a small shutter in the door, you’re beckoned into the villa by a well-dressed female halfling, **Regina**. Regina you recall is the head of household at the Villa, and Talurth's right-hand here in Waterdeep.
>
>Regina welcomes you in the foyer, she eyes your travel clothes meaningfully, giving a side-glance to a set of hooks and cubbies for your outer ware and boots. 
>
>***Give players a moment to discard their travel gear***
>
>Beyond the foyer, the villa opens up to several hallways and a staircase. Regina escorts you to a well-furnished parlor to the left.
>“Lord Talurth will be with you shortly,” says Regina. “Help yourselves to the pastries.”
>
>You are by yourself in the parlor, which is completely silent except for a ticking clock and the muffled sounds of voices coming from Talurth's office. The magnificent oak doors leading to the office are closed. A few moments pass.
>
>[[Billy Biscuits]], you have an odd tick when comes to closed doors. You eye them as a challenge. These doors you think could be knocked off their massive brass hinges in 4 shoulder charges... Maybe 3. Who do these doors think that they are? Hanging smugly on their hinges. These doors are proudly displaying their so-called power over you. You are out and you NEED to be in. Fuck these motherfucking smug doors. Your muscles tense and....
> 
>Your thought is interrupted as the doors swing open, and three men in dark suits march out of the office with Talurth behind them.  Talurth exclaims, "Safe travels. Give my best to Lord Drylund - he has my full and complete endorsement for Waterbaron. He must stop by when he is back shoreside".  One of the men turn around and drop a green felt pouch into Talurth's hand. Talurth eyes the bag in his hand, and clears his throat uncomfortably, "Ahh! I will be sure to visit Yatar myself, and sit with Drylund on his return".
>
>The men leave the parlor and Talurth retreats back into his office. The doors are left open.

## Bargewright Inn

![[tc_bargewright-inn.png|center 500]]

- Traveling to Bargewright Inn
	- Harshnag:
		- He has never had direct conflict or business with the Zhentarim. He is before for their time. But he knows the history of Bargewright Inn.
		- It was once a large legitimate inn on a hilltop overlooking the city Womford across the Dessarin River. The Inn is completely surrounded in an impressive wall. The only known entrance is through Womford city, which connects to Bargewright via a long wooden bridge known as The Ironford Bridge.
		- The Zhentarim, already well established in Triboar, where their legitimate business is headquartered - took ownership of the Inn and established the "Black Network" - their illegitimate business is headquartered here.
		- Everyone; including the law, are well aware, of Bargewright Inn - but do not interfere. Either have been bought off, or fear distribution to their city's supply line. Bargewright wholly governs itself.
		- Surrounding the Inn proper - is a maze of built and rebuilt ramshackle of towers, buildings and dwellings. All types of people settle here, and enjoy the protection and/or isolation from Faerun.
			- If parts of the town burn down, or are abandoned for whatever reason- new buildings are constructed on the outer perimeter of the city
			- The Zhent most likely encourage the maze like fashion to further discourage invaders.
			- Because of this, it is advised to stay clear of the ghetto sections. All sorts of rumors come out of these sections (likely spread or exaggerated by the Zhent)
		- The Black Network is operated by an unknown number of members that form a plutocracy.
		- The ranking known member of the Zhent here is the "Senior Sword" (Master of the Milita) - Chalaska Muruin
		- Zhent here may be evasive of their business, but have little fear of retaliation, so strangely forthcoming as well.
