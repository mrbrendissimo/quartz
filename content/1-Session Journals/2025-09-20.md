## SKT Intro 
- ### Giant History
	- Giants and Dragons ruled the world before smallfolk.
	- Went to war with Dragons. Never officially ended, but severely reduced their numbers, so war fizzled out.
	- Giants ruled from their capital city - Ostoria. Which is in the far north (no longer on maps). It was lost over time. Few ruins of the city can be visible.
	- Annam the All-Father is the giant god and established the Ordning.
		- He disowned the giants after they lost Ostoria. 
- ### The Ordning
	- Giant hiearchy/caste system.
	- Storm Giants are rulers. They live in fortresses under the sea and in the sky
	- Cloud giants take orders from Storm giants. Build floating castles on clouds
	- Frost Giants, Fire Giants, and Stone Giants take orders from Cloud Giants.
		- Frost > Knightly orders
		- Fire > Armory 
		- Stone > Infrastructure
	- Hill Giants are dullards and bottom of the food chain. They are bullies and vanguard. 
- ### Current Day 
	- Giants keep to themselves
	- Sittings are rare, but do occur
	- Not inherently evil, but not liked because of destruction due to their size
	- Rare for dragons and giants to get along
	- Goliaths love giants. Think they are part giant. Giants think Goliaths are gross, or view them as dogs to wolves.
	- [[Billy Biscuits]] is your giant lore encyclopedia. He also speaks giant.
